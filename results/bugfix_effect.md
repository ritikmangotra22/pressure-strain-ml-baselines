**Interpretation.** Direct effect: for models whose hyperparameters did not change, the change is ≤ 2 %
(classical, ridge, RF, and the unchanged LightGBM/MLP folds). Indirect effect: in folds where the
Re_τ = 180 case was a training case, the single extra point perturbed the inner tuning objective
slightly. TPE then followed a different path and picked different configurations in 5 of 20 tuned
model-fold combinations: LightGBM folds 1000, 2000 and 5200, and MLP folds 2000 and 5200. The
resulting test-error changes (LightGBM 4–9 %; MLP 2000: 0.032 → 0.041; MLP 5200: 0.220 → 0.167)
are larger than the seed-to-seed SD. They measure how sensitive the selected model is to small
perturbations of the tuning problem, and are reported as such in the Discussion. Only the
corrected results are used in the manuscript.
